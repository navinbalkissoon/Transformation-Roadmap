Disney Streaming Governance RACI

Member B Governance Designer: Nelson-Augusto Hernandez Panunzio
Member A Roadmap Sequencer and Site Editor: Navin Balkissoon
Member C Dashboard Designer: Eferhire Govan

This governance design supports Member A’s seven streaming initiatives, from BAMTech platform control to the planned unified Disney+ and Hulu app. It assigns a single final approver to recurring choices about funding, technology, data, and commercial execution.

The matrix is our proposed decision model, using Disney’s documented 2023 structure as a reference. It is not a claim that these exact approval rules existed throughout 2017 to 2026 or describe today’s organization. Disney centralized distribution in 2020 and restored segment authority in 2023, so approval rights need explicit review after reorganizations. [B1, B2, B3]

R = prepares and carries out the decision. A = final approver who owns the outcome. C = consulted before approval. I = informed afterward. Each row has exactly one A and at least one R.

3 Accountable assignment rationale

G1 Allocate and reallocate transformation funding

The CFO is accountable for reallocations within the authorized transformation budget because this role can compare investment needs across the portfolio and protect its financial limits. Segment leaders prepare the business cases, while material acquisitions or increases beyond delegated authority require the separate CEO and Board approval process.

Roadmap: C1, C2, W1, W2, R1, R2, R3. Review: Quarterly and when spending changes.

G2 Set portfolio priorities and settle authority conflicts

The CEO is accountable when Entertainment and ESPN compete for shared capacity or disagree over decision rights. This places the final tradeoff above either segment and provides a clear escalation route when reorganizations change who controls distribution or streaming results.

Roadmap: C2; W1, W2, R1, R2, R3; roadmap D2, D3, D4. Review: Quarterly and when dependencies or ownership change.

G3 Approve shared architecture standards and exceptions

The Head of Product and Technology is accountable because shared streaming infrastructure needs consistent engineering standards across services. This role can assess whether an exception creates integration, reliability, or migration risks before commercial teams commit to a delivery date.

Roadmap: C1; W1, W2, R3. Review: At each design review and exception request.

G4 Approve data quality and access policy across services

The General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer is accountable for the policy governing data quality obligations and permissible access across services. Technology and Platform Distribution implement validation and permissions, while legal review prevents advertising or app integration from expanding data use without an approved basis.

Roadmap: C1; W1, W2, R1, R2, R3. Review: At each new data use and quarterly policy review.

G5 Approve and renew streaming vendor and distribution partnerships

The designated Disney Entertainment cochair is accountable for Disney+ and Hulu commercial partnerships because those agreements affect the entertainment business’s economics and reach. Platform Distribution prepares the proposal, with Finance, Technology, and Legal checking cost, compatibility, rights, and exit terms before approval.

Roadmap: C1; W1, W2, R1, R3. Review: At selection, renewal, and material contract change.

G6 Approve Disney+ and Hulu pricing and advertising tradeoffs

The designated Disney Entertainment cochair is accountable because subscription prices and advertising choices jointly affect subscriber value and segment results. Advertising Sales and Platform Distribution prepare alternatives, while Finance and Technology evaluate revenue, churn risk, and delivery feasibility so ad revenue does not become the only objective.

Roadmap: W2, R1, R2, R3. Review: Before each pricing, bundle, or advertising policy change.

G7 Approve Disney+ and Hulu launch expansion and migration readiness

The designated Disney Entertainment cochair is accountable for Disney+ and Hulu rollout decisions because that role owns the commercial consequences of exposing customers to a release. Technology and Platform Distribution supply readiness evidence, including account access, billing accuracy, support preparation, and rollback capability, before the approver authorizes a wave.

Roadmap: W2, R1, R2, R3; W1 supplies earlier platform evidence. Review: Before every market launch or migration wave.

4 Decisions required this quarter

Illustrative planning checkpoint: calendar Q4 2025, after the August 2025 unified app announcement. These are proposed leadership requests for Member C’s dashboard, not reported Disney decisions or claims about completed results. Disney’s announcement identifies engagement, churn, efficiency, and advertising as expected benefits, not guaranteed outcomes. [B4]

G1 R3 Unified Disney+ and Hulu App

Approve a capped integration funding tranche rather than unrestricted rollout funding.

Accountable: CFO. Timing: Before the next funding release.

Evidence and condition: Cost estimate, staffing plan, milestone dates, and Finance review. If evidence is missing, defer the funding decision.

G4 R2 Ad-Supported Disney+ Tier and R3 Unified Disney+ and Hulu App

Approve the policy for shared account and viewing data, including which uses are permitted for advertising.

Accountable: General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer. Timing: Before new cross service data use.

Evidence and condition: Data definitions, access rules, reconciliation results, and privacy review. Without approval, retain existing access restrictions.

G7 R3 Unified Disney+ and Hulu App

Authorize the first migration wave only when mandatory readiness checks pass; otherwise defer it.

Accountable: Designated Disney Entertainment cochair. Timing: Before the first migration wave.

Evidence and condition: Test results for sign in, billing, entitlements, support readiness, and rollback. Member C should show evidence pending until verified.

5 Decision enforcement

The Responsible roles prepare the evidence and obtain input from every Consulted role before the Accountable role signs the decision log. The log records the decision, affected initiatives, rationale, conditions, and follow up date, then notifies Informed roles. The CEO resolves cross segment conflicts through G2; technical or legal clearance cannot be waived through a commercial launch approval. These controls are proposed for the project.
